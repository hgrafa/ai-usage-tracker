Download for Mac Release notes

macOS 15 or later. Free, open source.

Install

The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized, so macOS Gatekeeper will block a normal double-click the first time.

  1. 1

    Download AI-Usage.dmg.

  2. 2

    Open the disk image and drag AI Usage into the Applications folder.

  3. 3

    In Applications, right-click AI Usage and choose Open, then Open again in the dialog. After that it launches normally from the menu bar (no Dock icon).

What it shows

A percent in the menu bar for each provider you leave enabled.

Claude
How much of your Claude allowance you have used, with the session cookie stored in the Mac Keychain.
Codex
How much of your Codex allowance you have used, read from a local codex login.
Cursor
How much of your Cursor allowance you have used, with an optional token stored in the Mac Keychain.
Grok Bot
How much of your Grok Bot allowance you have used, reusing the Cursor login.

Secrets stay in the Keychain on this Mac. Nothing is sent to the author.
